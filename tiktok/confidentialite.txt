SLS Studio - Privacy Policy
Street Liftings (www.streetliftings.fr)

SLS Studio only connects to the Street Liftings TikTok account, with the permissions user.info.basic
and video.upload. It stores the TikTok access token locally on the Street Liftings team computer,
never shares it and never sells or transfers any data.

The only content sent to TikTok is the images and captions of Street Liftings posts. Competition
results come from public FinalRep results. No personal data of other TikTok users is collected.

Full privacy policy of Street Liftings: https://www.streetliftings.fr/politique-de-confidentialite/
