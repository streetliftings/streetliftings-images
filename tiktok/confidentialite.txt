SLS Studio - Privacy Policy
Streetliftings (www.streetliftings.fr)

SLS Studio only connects to the Streetliftings TikTok account, with the permissions user.info.basic
and video.upload. The TikTok access token is stored securely, never shared, and no data is ever sold or transferred.

The only content sent to TikTok is the images and captions of Streetliftings posts. Competition
results come from public FinalRep results. No personal data of other TikTok users is collected.

Full privacy policy of Streetliftings: https://www.streetliftings.fr/politique-de-confidentialite/
